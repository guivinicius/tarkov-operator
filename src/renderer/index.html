Welcome to Tarkov Operator

Get running in 4 steps

  1. Paste your API key in the Providers tab. OpenRouter and OpenAI are the easiest since one key covers both LLM and Speech-to-Text.
  2. Click Validate to confirm the key works.
  3. Click Enable Operator at the top of this window.
  4. Tap your push-to-talk key (F1) and ask a question out loud.

Microphone

Recording runs inside the app — no external audio tools to install. Your operating system asks for microphone permission the first time you use push-to-talk. If you denied it, grant it in your system privacy settings and restart the app.

Push-to-talk

Tap your PTT key once to start recording, then just stop talking — recording ends on silence. Tap again while speaking to cancel. Recording is capped at 30 seconds.
